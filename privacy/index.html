DSH Hello Privacy Policy

Effective date · 2026-11-23

1. Overview

DSH Hello is a mobile client for DeepSeek Harness (DSH). It connects only to a DSH Host that you choose and control. We do not operate your Host, and we do not receive the prompts, files, images, terminal output, or other content you send through it.

2. Data stored on your device

The app stores the Host address, the device pairing token, the device name, your preferences, session state, and cached files or images on your device. You can clear caches in Settings; uninstalling the app removes its local data.

3. Content sent to your Host

Messages, attachments, voice recordings, file requests, terminal input and output, and push registration are sent directly to the Host you configure. The Host operator (you, or a server you choose) controls that data, and their terms and privacy policy apply.

4. Push notifications

If you enable push notifications, a device push token is shared with your Host and with Google FCM (Android) or Apple Push Notification service (iOS) so notifications can be delivered. If your Host uses a push relay, the relay operator also processes that token.

5. Purchases

If the app offers in-app purchases, Google Play or the App Store processes the payment. We receive purchase status and receipt-validation data needed to unlock or restore features; we do not receive your payment card details. Purchases are tied to the store account used to buy them.

6. What we do not collect

The app has no developer account system, no developer-operated analytics, no advertising SDK, no telemetry, and no sale of personal data. Platform services such as Google Play services and Apple Push Notification service may collect diagnostic or device data under their own policies.

7. Third-party services

Your use of a Host, AI or model providers configured on that Host, Google Play services, Apple services, and the app stores is governed by their own terms and privacy policies. We do not control those services.

8. Security

Pairing tokens are stored locally on your device. Use HTTPS and a Host you trust. You are responsible for securing your Host, your credentials, and any data you choose to send.

9. Retention and deletion

Local app data remains until you clear it or uninstall the app. Cached media and files can be cleared in Settings. Data stored on your Host is controlled by the Host operator and is not deleted by uninstalling the app.

10. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, or the minimum age required in your country. Do not use it to send content that you are not allowed to share.

11. International transfers

Your content goes to the Host you choose. We do not transfer it to a developer-operated server. If you use a Host or AI provider in another region, that operator handles the transfer under its own policy.

12. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions about this policy can be sent to the contact address below.
